Privacy policy

Your privacy.

Your birth details and saved readings stay on your iPhone. Here’s how the app, its maps and this website handle information.

Effective 28 September 2026 · The Signal is provided by Russell William Kay, United Kingdom.

Your profile and readings

The name you enter, birth date, optional birth time, birthplace, selected receiver location, preferences and saved readings are stored in the app on your iPhone. Signal calculates its readings on the device. No Signal account is required. The developer does not operate a server that receives your profile or reading history.

Location and place names

If you allow location access, Signal uses your location while you use the app to show nearby places and shape a place-based reading. You can use a manually chosen place instead. Compass information is used to orient the receiver.

Apple’s location and geocoding services may handle coordinates when resolving a place name. These services are subject to Apple’s privacy information. You can change location permission at any time in iPhone Settings.

Mapbox maps

The interactive map uses Mapbox. Map requests and the Mapbox SDK may transmit approximate or precise location, a generated identifier, map interactions, usage information, performance data and other diagnostics. Mapbox uses this information for app functionality and analytics. Its SDK privacy declarations describe these categories as not linked to your identity and not used for tracking.

Signal retains Mapbox’s map attribution control. Open the information control on the map to review the available telemetry opt-out. Turning off optional telemetry does not prevent the network requests required to display maps.

Read Mapbox’s privacy policy and telemetry information for its practices, retention and choices. We do not send your birth details or saved readings to Mapbox.

Advertising and tracking

Signal has no advertising SDK. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising tracking. The current version has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Storage, backups and removal

Local profile data and saved readings remain on your device until you change them or remove the app’s data. You can edit your birth details in You. Deleting Signal from the iPhone removes its local app data; offloading the app keeps its documents and data.

Your local data may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings. Backups are controlled separately through Apple and may restore data when you restore the device or app. The developer cannot retrieve your local profile or reading history for you.

Support messages

The app’s suggestion and feedback links open an editable email draft. Signal does not automatically attach your birth details, saved readings or current location.

If you contact thesignalapp@outlook.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and investigate problems. Please share only what is necessary.

Support email is hosted by Microsoft Outlook and is subject to Microsoft’s privacy statement. We keep correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-up or meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies or contact forms. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Why information is processed

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process support correspondence to answer requests and provide the service, and rely on legitimate interests to troubleshoot problems and protect the service. Location access is controlled by your device permission. Mapbox, Apple, Microsoft and GitHub describe their own processing purposes, legal bases and international transfer safeguards in the policies linked above. Their services may process information outside your country.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information held about you, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact us about information in support correspondence. For data held only on your iPhone, use the app’s birth-details controls, device settings or app deletion.

You can also complain to your local data-protection authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, email thesignalapp@outlook.com. This policy will be updated here if the app’s practices change.
