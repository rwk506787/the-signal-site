Landscape traditions

Our Scottish connections reference David Furlong’s landscape pattern. The Michael alignment, Robert Twigger’s Great North Line and Gary Biltcliffe and Caroline Hoare’s Spine of Albion provide named connections for other lines.

Signal draws its own representative connections using independently sourced geographic points. We do not reproduce those authors’ maps, prose, coordinate tables or walking tracks. Our Mary Line is an owner-directed Signal interpretation. These lines express spiritual traditions and app interpretations, not scientifically measured forces or navigation routes. No endorsement is implied.
