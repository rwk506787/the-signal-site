A little help.

Find your way around The Signal.

Getting started

Add your birth date and birthplace. If you know your birth time, include it to see your Rising sign and more precise personal astrology. If you don’t, choose “I don’t know”.

Your daily Signal

Today holds your reading and its You, Sky and Place influences. Open a source to explore what shaped it. Use the date row to revisit recent saved readings.

Choosing a place

Open Place to explore the map. Searching or moving the map lets you browse. Use the receiver controls to choose whether to follow your location or stay in one place, then confirm the fixed receiver when prompted.

Location permission is optional. You can change it in iPhone Settings. Map geography needs an internet connection.

Changing your birth details

Open You, then Birth details. You can also open settings using the gear in the top-right corner. Your existing saved readings are kept.

Map or compass trouble

If map geography is missing, check your connection and reopen Place. The receiver can appear as a dot when a compass heading is unavailable. Keep the phone away from magnets and allow its compass to settle.

Your data

Your profile and readings are stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app is different and keeps its documents and data. iCloud or computer backups are managed separately through Apple.

Suggest a place or correction

In the app, open You → Settings, then choose “Suggest a place” or “Feedback or corrections”. This opens an email draft for you to edit and send. For a place suggestion, include its name, town or region and a useful website or map link if you have one.

Contact support

Email thesignalapp@outlook.com for help. When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Signal version and what happened. Avoid sending birth details or precise location unless they are needed to explain the problem.
