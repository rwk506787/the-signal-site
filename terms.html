Using The Signal.

Effective 28 September 2026 · Provided by Russell William Kay.

The app licence

The Signal is licensed under Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User Licence Agreement. That agreement governs your use of the app. This page explains the experience and where to find help.

A little perspective

The Signal offers spiritual interpretation through astrology, numerology and mapped place traditions. Readings are for reflection and personal exploration. They do not predict events or scientifically measure metaphysical forces, and are not medical, legal or financial advice.

Exploring places

Map entries describe real places and, where relevant, traditions or claimed alignments associated with them. A map entry does not establish public access, permission to enter or current visiting conditions. Check local information, respect private land and follow site rules.

Third-party services and credits

Maps are provided by Mapbox and its data sources. See our data credits and open geographic download. Map attribution is available in Place. Open You → Settings → About Signal → Acknowledgements for the app’s third-party notices.

Availability

The initial release is planned as a free iPhone app for iOS 17 or later, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Map geography needs an internet connection. Features that depend on location, compass information or birth time may vary with the information available.

Help and privacy

See Support for help using the app and our Privacy Policy for information about data handling. You can contact us at thesignalapp@outlook.com.
